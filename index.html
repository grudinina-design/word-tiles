How to play

Tap a cell to see its clue above the keyboard. At a crossing, tap the same cell again or use the direction button. Correct words turn green and lock automatically. Use Fit grid to get an overview. Select a cell to return to the larger grid.

Easy, Normal, and Hard start with five unlocked puzzles each. Every puzzle you solve in one of those categories unlocks the next one in that category.

Earn 3 hints the first time you solve a puzzle. Daily streak rewards are 3 hints on days 1–2, 5 on days 3–4, and 7 from day 5 onward. Missing a day restarts the streak. Revealing a letter costs 1 hint.
